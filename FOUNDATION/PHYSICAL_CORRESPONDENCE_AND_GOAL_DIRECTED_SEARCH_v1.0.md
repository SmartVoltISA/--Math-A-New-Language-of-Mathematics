# PHYSICAL CORRESPONDENCE & GOAL-DIRECTED SEARCH v1.0

Ω-Math separates structural objects from their physical interpretation. A mathematical candidate may be compared with known physical structures through invariants, relation patterns, transformation behavior, and derived properties.

\[
\text{Ω structure}\rightarrow\text{structural fingerprint}\rightarrow\text{physical pattern comparison}
\]

No name-based identification is permitted as proof. A match is a correspondence claim requiring empirical validation.

Unknown structural candidates may generate explicit `HYPOTHESIS` records when no known physical correspondence exists.

## Goal-directed structural search

A declared task can define constraints and objective properties for a search without prescribing the physical entity in advance:

\[
GOAL\rightarrow REQUIRED\ PROPERTIES\rightarrow STRUCTURAL\ SEARCH\rightarrow CANDIDATE\rightarrow TEST
\]

The goal and acceptance criteria must be fixed before search/execution. The search may discover structures with required behavior; physical interpretation remains a later correspondence step.

Thus Ω-Math supports both open discovery and task-directed design/search while preserving the distinction:

\[
entity\neq property\neq relation\neq law\neq measure\neq unit.
\]
