# Ω-Math Foundation — Entity → Property → Law → Measure → Unit

Status: FOUNDATIONAL METHODOLOGY v1.0

## Purpose

Ω-Math formalizes structure. It must not silently turn formal objects into physical entities or human measurement units.

The correct research order is:

`distinction → relation → boundary → entity-candidate → invariant → property → interaction/law → physical correspondence → measurement → unit`.

## Typed separation

Keep the following domains distinct:

`EntityState ≠ RelationState`.

Also keep distinct:

`entity ≠ property ≠ relation ≠ law ≠ measure ≠ unit`.

A numerical representation does not erase these type boundaries.

## Candidate entity

A formal candidate entity is a distinguishable, bounded relational organization whose identity can persist under a declared family of transformations through one or more invariants.

This is a formal research construct, not a claim that every such construct is a physical object.

## Invariants and identity

If two configurations differ but preserve the declared identity invariant,

`I(C₁)=I(C₂)`,

then they may represent the same candidate entity under the declared equivalence.

The equivalence must be explicit; it must not be inferred from visual resemblance.

## Properties

A property is a reproducible characteristic assigned to an entity or relation under a declared domain and operational definition.

Do not infer a physical property merely because a mathematical quantity has a familiar name.

## Laws

A mathematical identity, theorem or operator relation is not automatically a physical law.

Physical interpretation requires an explicit correspondence and empirical validation appropriate to the claim.

## Units

Units are measurement standards. They are not automatically primitives of the Ω-Math ontology.

Thus:

`physical duration ≠ second`

`physical length ≠ metre`

`physical mass ≠ kilogram`.

A dimensionless Ω quantity remains dimensionless until a justified physical correspondence introduces dimensional structure.

## Time

Ω-Math may represent order, transition, recurrence, relative intervals and dynamics. It must not silently identify transition count with physical duration.

The correct distinction is:

`transition count → structural temporal measure`

versus

`physical event interval → physical time measurement → unit`.

## Mental/model layer versus physical layer

A mathematical or mental model can contain structure and ordered transformations without containing physical time, mass, length or energy as ontological primitives.

Communication with the physical layer is a separate correspondence problem.

## Research rule

When a new structure resembles a known physical entity:

1. keep the formal structure unnamed or neutrally named;
2. derive its invariants and behavior;
3. test competing interpretations;
4. only then propose a physical correspondence;
5. only after physical correspondence is supported, discuss measurement and units.

## Core principle

> Find the entity before naming its property; establish the property before naming the law; establish the physical correspondence before assigning a measurement; assign the unit last.

This document does not change the declared mathematical primitives and does not claim a completed physical ontology.
