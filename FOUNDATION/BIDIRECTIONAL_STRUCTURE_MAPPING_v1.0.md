# BIDIRECTIONAL STRUCTURE MAPPING v1.0

Status: Ω-MATH METHODOLOGY

## Direction A — construct

`relations → structure → candidate entity → invariant → property`

## Direction B — abstract

`physical observation → observable property → relation → abstract structure → invariant → candidate entity`

Physical labels and measurement units are not primitives of the structural language.

## Goal-directed construction

A goal is represented as structural constraints/properties. The language searches candidate configurations that satisfy those constraints without importing the named physical solution.

## Convergence

Two independently generated signatures can be compared:

`Ω signature ↔ physical-derived signature`

Equivalent signatures support a correspondence hypothesis. They do not automatically establish that the abstract object is ontologically identical to a physical entity.

## Unknown correspondence

A robust unmatched signature remains `OPEN` or `HYPOTHESIS` until independently testable physical evidence exists.

## Typed separation

`entity ≠ property ≠ relation ≠ law ≠ measure ≠ unit`

This separation remains mandatory in both directions.
