# Ω-Math v0.9 — Quotient Geometry Conditions

## Purpose

This document corrects and makes explicit the status of quotient-induced distance. The formula below is a candidate construction; it is **not automatically a pseudometric for an arbitrary equivalence relation**.

## Base construction

Let `(X,d)` be a metric or pseudometric space and let `~` be an equivalence relation on `X`. For equivalence classes `[x]` and `[y]`, define

`d_Q([x],[y]) = inf { d(x',y') : x'~x, y'~y }`.

The value is well-defined as a function of the classes because the sets of representatives are determined by the classes. However, the metric axioms do not follow for an arbitrary equivalence relation.

## What is always safe

- `d_Q ≥ 0` when `d ≥ 0`.
- `d_Q([x],[x]) = 0`.
- symmetry follows when `d` is symmetric.
- separation of distinct classes is **not automatic**.
- triangle inequality is **not automatic** for an arbitrary equivalence relation.

Therefore the minimal language must call this a **quotient-distance candidate** until compatibility conditions are established.

## Sufficient compatible case

A standard sufficient setting is a quotient generated by a family of isometries (for example, a group action by isometries). In that case the induced orbit distance

`d_Q([x],[y]) = inf_g d(x,g·y)`

satisfies the triangle inequality and is a pseudometric on the orbit space. If distinct equivalence classes have strictly positive separation, it becomes a metric.

More generally, Ω-Math may admit any explicitly declared equivalence/correspondence family for which the quotient-distance axioms are proved or exhaustively verified on the declared finite domain.

## Finite verification rule

For a finite model, acceptance requires checking at minimum:

1. well-definedness on equivalence classes;
2. non-negativity;
3. symmetry when claimed;
4. identity of indiscernibles if metric status is claimed;
5. triangle inequality;
6. invariance under the declared equivalence/relabeling action.

A failed check downgrades the construction to a candidate/pseudodistance and records the counterexample.

## Ω-Math status

`QUOTIENT` remains a typed construction.

`d_Q` is a **derived quotient-distance candidate**.

It is promoted to pseudometric/metric only under explicit compatibility and separation conditions. No physical-space interpretation follows from this construction.

## Boundary

`quotient distance ≠ automatically metric`

`mathematical quotient geometry ≠ physical space`
