# Ω-Math Extension — Vector Relational Operators

**Status:** Conditional mathematical selection result
**Source lineage:** REL-PULSE-04

## Isotropic first-order ingredients

For a wavevector k in three dimensions, a translation-invariant rotationally covariant linear symbol can be constructed from isotropic tensors including `I`, `kkᵀ` and the cross-product operator `[k]×`.

The cross-product matrix satisfies

`[k]×ᵀ = -[k]×`

and

`[k]× k = 0`.

## Longitudinal/transverse decomposition

The vector k defines the longitudinal direction. The plane orthogonal to k is the transverse subspace.

The curl-type symbol leaves the longitudinal component at zero principal frequency while acting nontrivially on the transverse plane.

The spectrum of `i[k]×` is

`{-|k|, 0, +|k|}`.

## Selection statement

Within the restricted class

`real vector field + translation invariance + rotational isotropy + local first spatial order + positive quadratic conservation + exactly transverse propagating principal modes`,

the curl sector is strongly selected up to coefficient/sign and normalization.

## Countermodels

A longitudinal identity/gradient sector can propagate longitudinal components.

A fixed preferred-axis skew operator can conserve a quadratic quantity and oscillate but breaks rotational isotropy.

Higher-order factors such as `f(|k|²)[k]×` preserve transverse structure, showing why first spatial order is needed for principal-order selection.

## Boundary

This does not derive Maxwell's equations, electromagnetic fields, Lorentz invariance, the value of c, gauge symmetry or photon quantization. It is a conditional mathematical selection result.
