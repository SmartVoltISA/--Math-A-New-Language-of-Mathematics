`T³ + sufficiently regular fields + nonzero Fourier sector + transverse restriction + ω ≠ 0`,

`P = ω(J ⊗ curl)`

`invariant restriction ≠ kernel removal ≠ quotient ≠ boundary/topology choice`.

The theorem uses an invariant transverse restriction together with explicit removal of the zero Fourier mode. It does not silently replace these operations by a universal quotient construction.
