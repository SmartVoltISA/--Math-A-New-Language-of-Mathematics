## Closure rules

1. Every admitted object has an explicit type.
2. Every admitted operator has a declared input/output signature or framework status.
3. Every overloaded primitive value is separated by type.
4. Relation absence is not encoded as a relation value.
5. Paths are not replaced by scalar summaries.
6. Reduction is accepted only under task-relative sufficiency.
7. Deterministic finite behavior has an explicit behavioral quotient construction.
8. Nondeterministic branching is retained explicitly; no arbitrary successor selection is allowed.
9. Structural geometry is derived from declared transformations and costs.
10. Internal transition order is separated from physical duration.
11. Memory requires demonstrated future functional effect.
12. Causal claims require declared intervention semantics.
13. Emergence requires explicit coarse-graining and a declared criterion.
14. Physical interpretations are never implicit.

## Verified boundaries

The repository contains explicit counterexamples showing:

- sign-product can erase intermediate organization;
- equal source-distance profiles need not determine graph structure;
- structural similarity need not imply behavioral equivalence;
- collapsing nondeterministic branches to one successor can change behavior.

These counterexamples are boundary conditions, not failures of the language. They determine what Ω-Math refuses to identify without additional semantics.

## What “complete” means here

Ω-Math v0.8 is complete as a typed formal language for its admitted finite relational domain and its declared derived constructions. Completeness means that the language has explicit primitives, types, semantics, operator inventory, admissibility rules, reduction rules, verification boundaries and status discipline.

It does **not** mean:

- a proof that Ω-Math is the unique foundation of mathematics;
- a proof that relations are the ontology of nature;
- a derivation of physical space from the formal geometry;
- a derivation of physical time from transition order;
- a completed theory of probability or energy;
- a universal theory of emergence or consciousness;
- a physical theory with independent experimental predictions.

## Remaining research program

The remaining items are deliberately isolated rather than hidden:

1. canonical primitive relation composition;
2. canonical path equivalence;
3. infinite-horizon behavioral semantics;
4. full task taxonomy for nondeterministic equivalence;
5. continuous-state extension;
6. probabilistic dynamics;
7. causal/interventional semantics;
8. physical time;
9. energy;
10. physical ontology and independent predictions.

No item is silently promoted to the core.

## Final methodological rule

`define → derive → implement → execute → verify → compare → falsify → record`

A result becomes part of the language only at the status supported by its evidence.
