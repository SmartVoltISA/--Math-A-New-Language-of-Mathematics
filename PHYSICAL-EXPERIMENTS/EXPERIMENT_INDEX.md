## Rule

No experiment is considered evidence until it has raw data, preregistration, controls, held-out prediction, and independent replication.

## Expected hypothesis map

- serial travel-time composition → min-plus;
- linear transfer/superposition → sum-product;
- bottleneck capacity → max-min.

These are expected model-class results, not claims of universal physical truth.
