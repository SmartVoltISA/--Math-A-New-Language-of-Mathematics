# Ω-Math — Boundary Hypothesis v0.1

## Purpose

Test whether a boundary can arise as a derived consequence of increasing internal closure rather than being introduced as a primitive geometric surface.

This is a hypothesis, not a physical claim.

## 1. Starting structure

A finite system is:

`Ω=(E,R,D_R)`

Partition the current representation into three regions only for measurement:

- `I` — interior;
- `B` — candidate boundary;
- `X` — exterior.

The partition is a measurement device, not a new primitive of Ω-Math.

## 2. Closure

Let:

`R_II` = relations internal to `I`;
`R_IB` = relations between `I` and `B`;
`R_BX` = relations between `B` and `X`;
`R_IX` = direct relations between `I` and `X`.

Define the measured internal-closure ratio:

`C_in = R_II / (R_II + R_IB + R_IX)`

and external coupling ratio:

`C_out = (R_IB + R_BX + R_IX) / R_total`

These are candidate measurements, not primitive quantities.

## 3. Candidate boundary

A boundary is considered emergent only if a subset `B` simultaneously satisfies:

1. high internal retention relative to direct transmission;
2. persistent separation between interior and exterior;
3. non-zero coupling to the exterior;
4. stable or recurrent behavior under perturbation;
5. the boundary cannot be removed without changing the measured behavior of the system.

Thus a boundary is not merely a set of nodes selected after the fact.

## 4. Two directions

The Point hypothesis is tested through two independent tendencies:

### Inward

`C_in -> 1`

External connectivity decreases while internal relational closure increases.

### Outward

A non-zero boundary channel remains:

`R_BX > 0`

and may carry measurable state/energy-like flow once such a quantity is independently defined.

The existence of an outward channel must not be assumed from the word "boundary".

## 5. Information distinction

Internal identity/state distinctions may remain even when external observation merges them:

`e_i != e_j`

while

`O(e_i) = O(e_j)`.

Therefore external information loss does not imply destruction of internal structure.

A candidate boundary may act as a compression/quotient interface between internal and external descriptions.

## 6. Geometry is downstream

No area, distance, radius, dimension, or metric is assumed at this stage.

If repeated relational boundaries produce an invariant quantity that behaves like a surface measure, geometry may be introduced later as a derived representation.

Target sequence:

`relations -> closure -> separation -> boundary -> invariant -> geometry`

not:

`geometry -> boundary -> interpretation`.

## 7. Null controls

At minimum compare:

- random rewiring with preserved edge count;
- degree-preserving rewiring;
- label permutations;
- systems with high internal density but no persistent boundary;
- systems with an imposed boundary but no emergent separation.

A boundary claim is rejected if comparable behavior appears under null controls without the proposed closure mechanism.

## 8. Physical interpretation

No identification with a black hole is made here.

The word `Point` refers only to the hypothesized limiting relational regime:

`internal closure -> maximal`

`external coupling -> minimal but potentially non-zero`

`internal distinguishability -> not required to vanish`.

Any later correspondence with gravitational horizons, black-hole thermodynamics, radiation, or other physical phenomena must be established independently.
